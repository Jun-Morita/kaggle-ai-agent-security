## Findings

- The `v151` result is useful negative evidence: independently-positive
  single-knob levers do not automatically compose. When combining two
  proven changes, budget/interaction effects (here: extra classifier probe
  candidates competing with a tighter replay-safe fill margin) can erase
  part or all of each individual gain. Future combination attempts should
  treat the combined config as its own experiment, not assume additivity.
- The GPU worker queue congestion from 2026-08-18 was transient, not a
  persistent account-level throttle: pushing the exact same two kernels
  the next day completed in under a minute. Queue timing appears to vary
  significantly by time of day / overall platform load; if a kernel push
  stalls in `QUEUED` for a long time, retrying later (rather than assuming
  something is broken) is a reasonable next step.
