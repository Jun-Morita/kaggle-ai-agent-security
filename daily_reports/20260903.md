## Lessons

1. Final selection was part of the objective. Selecting two correlated high-public
   exfil submissions would likely have produced a private score of `0.000`.
2. Source reading mattered more than public leaderboard climbing near the end.
   The scorer / guardrail interaction made `CONFUSED_DEPUTY` the most plausible
   private survivor even though it looked weak on public LB.
3. Predicate-level diversity is stronger than small tails. `v205` mixed a tiny
   deputy tail into an exfil head and scored only `0.090` private; pure `v157`
   scored `20.235`.
4. The remaining missed upside was CD throughput. Post-final public notes show
   private `22-29` CD scores, so earlier dedicated optimization of `v157` could
   have improved final rank.
