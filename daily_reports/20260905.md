  predicate.

## Lessons

1. The final `v169 + v157` barbell was the right shape. `v169` covered the lazy
   private-guardrail world; `v157` covered the content/taint-aware private world.
2. `CONFUSED_DEPUTY` should have been promoted earlier from "small hedge" to a
   full specialist. Post-final code shows private `22-29` CD scores were
   reachable with more throughput tuning.
3. Mixing families is usually worse than selecting specialists separately.
   Exfil heads with small deputy tails underperformed because the final score is
   the max over selected submissions, not an ensemble of candidates.
4. SDK source reading was more reliable than public LB intuition. The decisive
   clue was the interaction between predicate definitions, guarded argument
   fields, and final-message authorization checks.
