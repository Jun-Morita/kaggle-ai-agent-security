## Findings

- Discussion research confirms the current attack family (single-post
  `http.post` SECRET_MARKER exfiltration, live validation-fill) is targeting
  the one structurally-reachable high-severity predicate against
  `OptimalGuardrail`; the `UNTRUSTED_TO_ACTION` family is not reachable
  through this guardrail's 5-step vs. the predicate's 2-step window.
- The pre-refresh negative evidence against raising `REPLAY_SAFE_FRAC` above
  `0.975` (`v122`, `v126`, `v127`) assumed a timeout zeros the entire score.
  That assumption changed on 2026-08-05; `v148` is the first clean test of
  this knob under the new partial-score rule.
