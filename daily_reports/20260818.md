## Findings

- Kaggle's GPU worker queue enforces an account-wide limit of 2 concurrent
  GPU kernel sessions; pushing a 3rd kernel while 2 are active/queued fails
  outright (`Kernel push error: Maximum batch GPU session count of 2
  reached.`), and even within that limit, queue wait times can run well
  past 40-50 minutes during busy periods. Plan submission batches around
  this constraint rather than pushing 3 kernels back-to-back.
- Public leaderboard scores among near-identical or copied notebooks are
  not independent evidence: `foysalemonshanto/ai-agent-security-v15`'s
  displayed `91.305` and `nikitagajbhiye30/ai-security-0011` v20's
  displayed `89.280` are the same underlying code, and neither reproduced
  above `v143=82.980` in our hosted draw. Always diff a newly-found
  high-scoring notebook's extracted `attack.py` by SHA-256 against
  previously-transferred sources before treating it as a fresh lead.
